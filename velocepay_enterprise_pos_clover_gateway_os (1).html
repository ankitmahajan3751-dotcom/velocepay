How to Test VelocePay & Clover Integration

Step-by-step sandbox walkthrough for all modules & payment gateways

1. Testing Clover POS Gateway

Click the Clover Integration tab in the top header or sidebar. Inspect the connected Clover Mini terminal (S/N: C025UT89104). Tap any catalog item (e.g., Artisan Double Espresso or Sourdough Avocado Toast) to instantly push a payment intent to the Clover terminal, record a real-time transaction in the ledger, and inspect webhook callbacks!

2. Secure Login, MFA & RBAC

On the login screen, use the 1-Click Demo Role Credentials pills (Super Admin, Merchant Owner, or POS Cashier) to instantly populate credentials. Click "Continue to 2FA", verify the auto-filled 6-digit TOTP code, and unlock the portal.

3. Real Card Payments & 3DS2 Challenge

Navigate to Real Card Payments. Use the preset test cards (Visa, Mastercard, Interac, Amex) to auto-fill valid Luhn card numbers with live BIN issuer detection.

  • Set amount to $125.00 (amounts $\ge \$100$ trigger the 3D Secure 2.2 Bank Challenge modal).
  • Test contactless payments by clicking Android NFC Tap in the top header.

4. Android PWA & Offline Store-and-Forward Queue

Click the ONLINE button in the top header to toggle offline mode. Notice the yellow offline banner appear. Process any card charge—it will safely queue in IndexedDB. Click Reconnect Now to test automatic store-and-forward sync.
